# From shortlist to host working copy

October 28, 2026. A separate reviewer started with no conversation history and assessed published 85e7c12 using the actual app, public source, film, official rules and prepared text. Its subjective baseline score was 5.25/10, with its lowest score for unvalidated venue usefulness. This is a critical software/product review, not venue research or a competition forecast.

## The central correction

Correct shortlist arithmetic does not establish a coherent finished event. Common Room now explicitly proposes a shortlist. The host chooses a passage or written introduction, an actual recording, a film focus and a connecting question. Those choices enter the displayed running order, printable copy and Markdown/JSON program. An unfinished copy retains a preparation checklist. Host-authored interpretation remains separate from Qloo ranks and catalogue metadata.

The app exposes its equal-slot and equal-circle assumptions. The threshold is a comparison rule, not a validated success measure. No attendance, satisfaction, revenue, time saving or superiority over a host/LLM is claimed. A real venue pilot remains future work.

## Concrete repairs

- Confirmed identity-choice stops reconcile workflows never started, retaining all started workflows. Timeout, schema and uncertain execution failures remain conservative. Hourly and lifetime caps were not raised or reset.
- The UI now shows the backend's actual expiry-derived quota message and shared-use caveat.
- Identity choices apply only to their originating circle and anchor line. Distinct returned identities include a short ID. Original entered labels, resolved names/types/UUIDs and workflow sources travel together in new exports.
- Optional Qloo catalogue descriptions, year and rating are bounded display data with a source link to the retained evidence. Missing metadata is explicit. External research links search names and do not claim a verified external identity.
- Tab-local save/reopen retains the brief, last completed result, identity choices and host notes. A restored result keeps its original time, is visibly labeled and makes no provider call. Invalid saved data fails closed. A failed rerun retains the earlier completed artifact.
- Host fields expand to fit readable notes. The print artifact separates shortlist/evidence, host choices and running order. Portable exports compile the actual chosen question and recording rather than leaving a conflicting generic prompt in the program.

The core suite has 59 tests. Fourteen earlier browser checks, eight round-three checks and eight targeted round-four checks cover their stated fixture, injected-error and save/reopen scopes.
